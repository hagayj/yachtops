Delete your Yacht Ops account

Yacht Ops users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address associated with your Yacht Ops account.
  2. Use the subject Yacht Ops account deletion request.
  3. Include your full name and the email address used to sign in.
Email a deletion request

We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request. We will acknowledge the request and complete it within 30 days.

Data that will be deleted

  • Your authentication account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your personal profile, contact details, notification token and preferences.
  • Personal documents and profile images associated only with your account.
  • Your membership links to Yacht Ops projects and vessels.

Data that may be retained

Shared vessel, voyage, maintenance and safety records may be retained in a de-identified form for operational continuity. Records required for legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes may be retained only for the period required by law. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 additional days before automatic removal.

Request deletion of specific data

You may use the same email process to request deletion of specific personal data without deleting your entire account. Describe the data you want removed in your message.
